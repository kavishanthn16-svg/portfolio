Featured Project

FleetPro

Vehicle Fleet Management System

FleetPro is a vehicle fleet management system focused on driver and staff readiness, records, leave handling, certifications, document validation, and operational oversight for fleet operations.

Role
Driver & Staff Management Module Developer
Stack
Next.js, React, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL
Auth / Deployment
JWT, AWS, Vercel
Storage
Supabase Storage where applicable

Problem

Managing driver and staff readiness requires accurate operational data.

The module needed to support driver and staff records, availability, leave, license and certification status, expiry monitoring, and qualification or eligibility validation.

Solution

A focused module for staff records, validation, and fleet readiness.

The solution connects frontend workflows with backend APIs, business validation, database persistence, and document storage for driver and staff management.
